About the hotel

Tirich Mir View Hotel is on Jamia Masjid Road in Chitral Town — the most-reviewed hotel in Chitral with 603 Google reviews. Guests come for mountain-view balconies, garden, terrace, and restaurant, and for easy access to Kalash valleys, Garam Chashma, and the Shandur Polo Festival.

Nearby

  • Garam Chashma — hot springs
  • Shandur Pass — Polo Festival
  • Chitral town bazaar, Jamia Masjid
